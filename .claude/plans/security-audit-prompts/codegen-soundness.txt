**Routed finding K1-6 (High, from the crypto-k1 stage, PR #375) — fix it in this stage.** Under `--number-mode i32`, `a.length` truncates an array's i64 length to i32 (`src/emit-arrays.ts:1098`, `emitArrayLength` -> `emitNumberFromI64`), and nothing stops an array growing past 2^31 - 1 elements (`runtime/runtime.c:1164`, `nish_array_grow`). For a 2^32 + 5 byte `u8[]`, `length` reads 5, `sha256` of it equals `sha256` of its first 5 bytes, `hmacSha256Verify` accepts the 5-byte message's tag, and `timingSafeEqual` against a 5-byte array answers true. Suggested fix: make `.length` panic instead of truncating under i32 mode (this stage owns src/emit-arrays.ts), with a failing-first link test. The runtime-c stage owns runtime/runtime.c; do not edit it — if an allocation-side refusal is also needed, record it as open for that stage.
