# Renumber Buffers and handles across `declare function` to WP53

## Why

`docs/roadmap/index.md` lists two WP40 rows: *Dependency capabilities* and
*Buffers and handles across `declare function`*. The second moves to WP53, the
next free number after WP52 (Fast rebuilds for `nish run`, itself renumbered from
a duplicate WP48 in #1393). WP40 keeps Dependency capabilities, which owns the
`docs/wp40-dependency-capabilities.md` note and every `WP40 §2a` / grants /
lockfile / sandbox reference in `src/`, `runtime/` and `tests/link/caps_*`.

## Stage: renumber

**Owns:** `docs/wp40-ffi-buffers-handles.md`, `docs/wp53-ffi-buffers-handles.md`,
`docs/roadmap/wp40-ffi-buffers-handles.md`, `docs/roadmap/wp53-ffi-buffers-handles.md`,
`docs/**/*.md` (only lines that point at or name the FFI package),
`docs/reference/**` (regenerated only), `scalar.config.json`, `src/**/*.ts`
(comments only), `std/**` (comments only), `runtime/**` (comments only),
`tests/**` (comments, `tests/wordings/unreachable.txt`,
`tests/differential/known-failures.txt` notes only), `tests/self/goldens/checked/**`
(regenerated only).

**Not owned:** `changelog/**`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `.claude/plans/**`.

### Classifying a bare "WP40"

The FFI package's own vocabulary: stages **B1.1–B1.3** (byte buffers),
**H1.1–H1.2** (handle classes), sections **§3a–§3c, §4, §4a, §5**, question
**Q12**, and words like *buffer*, *`u8[]` lent to C*, *handle class*, *`CPtr`
field*, *`using` closes*. Those become WP53.

Dependency capabilities' vocabulary: **§2a**, **§6**, *grants*, *dependencies*,
*capability lockfile*, *`--update-capability-lock`*, *exec allowlist*, the
`tests/link/caps_dep_*` and `caps_lock_*` programs, `runtime/runtime-sandbox.c`.
Those stay WP40.

A mention that is genuinely ambiguous goes in the PR body with its reading, not
guessed silently. A `§` number cited beside WP53 keeps its value: the note's
sections are not renumbered, only the package.

### What did #1393 touch

8 files, 9 lines: the two renamed files, MASTER_PLAN, README, roadmap index,
`docs/security/cli.md`, `scalar.config.json`, one `src/` comment. This stage is
the same shape with a much larger label surface (~40 `src/` comment lines,
~150 test-case header comments, ~30 doc lines), because the FFI package has
been cited by number throughout the compiler and its tests.
